# Multi-photo groups

Several photos or videos as one beat. On/off is always **Motion → Transitions Mix → Multi-photo**. Sections below that hold cadence, count, and direction.

![3D Ribbon and Carousel cadence](../.gitbook/assets/inspector-motion-groups.png)

New projects: **Photo Stacks, Carousel, and 3D Ribbon on**; **Filmstrip**, **Perspective Pair**, and **Scatter & Settle** off until you enable them or pick a Look that opts in. Looks set their own cadence.

Groups keep at least **two** normal slides between windows. Carousel, 3D Ribbon, and Filmstrip also keep at least **four** normal slides between consecutive *travel* windows.

Once you rearrange slides by hand, windows are **pinned** to those photos. Sorting, shuffling, resetting, or changing cadence / count hands placement back to the planner.

## Photo Stacks

- **Stack Every** — 3…8 (default 4)
- **Stack Size** — 3, 4, or 5 cards (default 4)

Fanned cards; faded Ken Burns washes behind.

## Carousel

- **Carousel Every** — 3…8 (default 4)
- **Photos** — 3, 4, or 5 seats (default 5)
- **Direction** — Left to Right / Right to Left / Random

Cards join a turning ring, one revolution, then peel off.

## 3D Ribbon

- **Ribbon Every** — 3…8 (default 5)
- **Photos** — 4 / 5 / 6 (default 5)
- **Direction** — Left to Right / Right to Left / Random

Open belt; one hero at a time, flanks beside it.

## Perspective Pair

- **Pair Every** — 3…8 (default 6)
- Always **two** photos

Cover-flow deck: front advances, then flies aside as the back becomes the hero.

## Filmstrip

- **Filmstrip Every**
- **Photos** — 4 / 5 / 6
- **Direction** — Horizontal / Vertical / Random

Flat perforated strip; centre hero at Photo Size. Distinct from Ribbon (depth belt) and Carousel (orbit).

## Scatter & Settle

- **Scatter Every** — 3…8 (default 6)
- **Photos** — 3…6 (default 5)
- **Direction** — From Left / From Right / Random
- **Spread** — Wide or Tight
- **Throw** — Mild / Medium / Full
- **Speed** — Gentle / Normal / Snappy

Cards throw in, land scattered, come forward in timeline order, then throw off the other side.

## Timeline / Library

Follower slides collapse onto the lead cell. Library badges: **stack 1/5**, **carousel 2/5**, **ribbon 5/5**, **pair 1/2**, **filmstrip 3/5**. Videos that fit a window play through it; unmuted clip audio is audible only while that card is the hero (same idea on Filmstrip / Scatter).
