# Looks

Inspector → **Style**. A Look is the movie’s aesthetic: grade, border, stage, backdrop, lens deal, Photo Size, transition mix bias, Match Look music, and which multi-photo groups run.

![Eight Look chips](../.gitbook/assets/inspector-looks.png)

![Energy, Stage, Photo Size under Look](../.gitbook/assets/inspector-masters.png)

**⌘Z** undoes Style changes. **Reset Style to Defaults** restores this tab (and caption *style*, not caption *text*). Once captions exist, that control becomes a menu: **Reset styles only** or **Reset styles and clear [N] captions…**.

## The eight chips

| Look | In the app |
| --- | --- |
| **Clean** | Nothing between you and the photo. White matte, Gentle cuts. |
| **Polaroid** | Instant prints. Fat white matte, Playful bias. |
| **Vintage** | Aged album. Heavy black mat, torn edge, Gentle cuts. |
| **Cinematic** | Anamorphic / locked off. Dramatic cuts, widest lens deal. |
| **Noir** | Moody true-mono. Gentler than crushed blacks; Gentle cuts. |
| **B&W** | Soft documentary grayscale. Cleaner than Noir. |
| **Golden Hour** | Warm late-day colour. Gentle cuts. |
| **Crisp** | Cool editorial snap. Dramatic cuts. |

Clicking a chip re-populates **Motion → Transitions Mix**, re-deals single-slide cuts, and applies that Look’s group settings. Hand-picked Motion checkboxes are discarded.

Editing **Customize…**, **Stage**, **Photo Size**, or **Motion** switches the chip to **Custom**.

Looks never turn **Atmosphere** or **Decals** on. Those stay **None** until you pick them under Customize.

Energy, output format, intro on/off, intro text, a background still you already chose, and per-photo overrides are not reset by a Look click. A Look **does** skin the intro card.

## Energy

Header band: **Calm · Steady · Lively · Energetic · Intense**. Slider from **Calm** to **Intense**. Biases motion and pacing.

## Stage

**Dark** (black void) or **Light** (cream gallery). Behind every plate, wash, seam, and the end fade. Changing Stage yourself also marks Style as Custom.

## Photo Size

Word labels only: **Smallest · Small · Medium · Large · Largest** (not a percentage readout). Default for every Look is **Large**. Sets hero / single-slide short-edge fill. Group heroes take Photo Size into account; a group may soft-fit under the label so it stays on stage — single-slide heroes are not flattened to spare a group.

## Captions on this tab

Bulk Auto Caption / Clear, and (Studio) **Type & Placement**. See [Intro and captions](../intro-captions.md).
