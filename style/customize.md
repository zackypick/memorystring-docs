# Customize

Studio only. Inspector → **Style** → **Customize** (open by default in Studio). Each inner block starts **collapsed**:

**Plate · Ambience · Lens Effects · Film · Atmosphere & Decals**

![Customize disclosures at the bottom of Style](../.gitbook/assets/inspector-style.png)

A Look that ticks several lens boxes still plays **at most one** pooled effect per photo. Clicking the **same Look chip again** deals a fresh three (except **Clean**). See [Looks](looks.md).

## Plate

- **Photo Border** — White or Black matte
- **Border Width** and **Border Opacity**
- **Torn Edge** + strength — Soft deckle or Rough hand-torn (when not off)
- **Paper Curl**
- **Drop Shadow** — Soft or Strong
- **Rim Glow** — Soft or Strong
- **Corners** — Rounded / Sharp

## Ambience

- **Floor Reflection** — Soft or Strong
- **Glow on Entry**
- **Wind** + **Wind Direction** (when Wind is on)
- **Light Leak**
- **Backdrop** — Grayscale / Colored / Random
- **Backdrop Seat** — Centered / Offset

The backdrop is a blurred, enlarged copy of the photo. **Random** is stable per photo seed — preview matches export.

## Lens Effects

Optical light. Checkboxes (A–Z in the Inspector):

Anamorphic Streaks, Bokeh, Flare, Ghosting, Orbs, 50mm Prime, Pulse, Refract Bubbles, Sparkle, Starburst, Sweep, Veiling Glare, Vignette

Plus **Randomize Selected** and **How often**.

A photo plays **at most one** pooled lens effect. **Randomize Selected** on (how Looks leave it): equal odds among checked boxes; **How often** (default 70%) is how many photos get one at all. Off: Vignette and optical accents can paint on every photo instead of taking the slot (Refract Bubbles puts them back in one draw).

Anamorphic Streaks and Refract Bubbles have extra knobs and presets when those boxes are on.

**Studio:** right-click a Timeline or Library slide → **Lens Effect** (under Slide Transition) to pin effects on that slide or group. Pins survive Look re-deals.

## Film

**Film Grain**, **Color Fringe**, **Film Scratches**, each with amount. Outside the lens pool — never thinned out by Randomize.

With a photo selected, **This photo** can set grain / fringe / scratches to Inherit, Off, or On. Per-photo film edits do **not** switch Style to Custom.

## Atmosphere & Decals

Each menu is a **single choice**. Default **None**. Looks never set these.

- **Atmosphere** — None · Bubbles · Leaves
- **Decals** — None · Travel · Vacation · Party · Florals · Wedding · Pets · Sports

Travel / Vacation include a dotted route plane. There is no separate Map or Route checkbox.
