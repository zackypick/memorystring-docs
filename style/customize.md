# Customize

**Studio** → Inspector → **Style** → **Customize** fine-tunes plate, ambience, lens, film, and decorations. Sections start collapsed:

**Plate · Ambience · Lens Effects · Film · Atmosphere & Decals**

![Customize disclosures at the bottom of Style](../.gitbook/assets/inspector-style.png)

Even if several lens boxes are checked, each photo plays **at most one** pooled lens effect. Click the same **Look** again for a new random pick (except **Clean**). See [Looks](looks.md#the-eight-chips).

**Reset Style to Defaults** restores this tab. **⌘Z** undoes. Photos, music, order, and trims stay.

## Plate

Mat and card around the photo.

- **Photo Border** — **White** or **Black** matte
- **Border Width** and **Border Opacity**
- **Torn Edge** + strength
- **Paper Curl**
- **Drop Shadow** — Soft or Strong
- **Rim Glow** — Soft or Strong
- **Corners** — Rounded / Sharp

## Ambience

Effects around the plate.

- **Floor Reflection** — Soft or Strong
- **Glow on Entry**
- **Wind** — **None**, **Gentle Wind**, **Light Wind**
- **Wind Direction** — when Wind is on
- **Light Leak**
- **Backdrop** — Grayscale / Colored / Random
- **Backdrop Seat** — Centered / Offset

Backdrop is a blurred enlarged photo. Groups crossfade washes every few cards.

| Backdrop | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Grayscale** | Wash is mono |
| **Colored** | Wash keeps photo colors |
| **Random** | About half mono / half color, stable per photo |

## Lens Effects

Optical light on the frame. Full list and clips: **[Lens effects](lens-effects.md)**.

Checkboxes include **Anamorphic Streaks, Bokeh, Flare, Ghosting, Orbs, 50mm Prime, Pulse, Refract Bubbles, Sparkle, Starburst, Sweep, Veiling Glare, Vignette**, plus **Randomize Selected** and **How often**. At most one pooled effect per photo.

## Film

Texture outside the lens pool.

- **Film Grain** + **Grain Amount**
- **Color Fringe** + **Fringe Amount**
- **Film Scratches** + **Scratch Amount** — on backdrop only

With a photo selected, **This Photo** can set **Inherit**, **Off**, or **On** per control.

## Atmosphere & Decals

Optional weather and edge motifs. Default **None**. Looks do not set these.

- **Atmosphere** — **None** · **Bubbles** · **Leaves**
- **Decals** — **None** · **Travel** · **Vacation** · **Party** · **Florals** · **Wedding** · **Pets** · **Sports**

Effects stay at the edges, not covering the main photo.
