# What's new

MemoryString is a free slideshow app for Mac. Here is what comes next, and a one-line headline for every version that has already shipped. Bug fixes for each release are on [Fixed](fixed.md).

## Up next

**1.2.9.1** More realistic effects, and a new multi-card perspective transition.

**1.2.9.2** A lens pass: upgrades to the lens effects, in its own version.

## Shipped

Newest first.

**1.2.8** Faster Look clicks, cleaner Refractive Bubbles, and Fit Show to Music that leaves Music Follows Show on.

**1.2.7** A new 50-track CC0 soundtrack, backdrop zoom and aim, and Golden Hour that stays warm through the exit card.

**1.2.6** Closing the window quits the app, and Settings opens as a sheet on the main window.

**1.2.5** Long story short rebuilds start clean, and a full-body photo keeps the place in the blur.

**1.2.4** Fit Show to Music, opening and closing titles, Peek, and Studio fonts.

**1.2.2** Mute noisy videos, a smaller music catalog, and a Long story short import that no longer gets stuck.

**1.2.1** Long story short goes up to 30 minutes, and the finished movie lands within about 10 seconds of the length you pick.

**1.2.0** A full-bleed intro that follows the stage, backdrops that never mirror, and transitions you can check and count.

**1.1.3** The Long story short Duration slider matches what the photos can fill, plus smoother play and scrub, and fixes for a stuck timeline, missing music on a new show, and a doubled re-import count.

**1.1.2** Imports no longer hang on HEIC photos, and the original shot date stays with each photo.

**1.1.1** Photos imports no longer get stuck at Importing N of M.

**1.1.0** Long story short builds a shareable movie from a big pile of photos and videos, with richer Caption Details.

**1.0.2** Close and New reset style, Scatter matches Photo Size, and export reveals the movie in Finder.

**1.0.1** The app starts empty on launch and warns before a large import.

**1.0.0** Photos import, Outtakes, and Auto Trim.
