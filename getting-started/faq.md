# FAQ

<details>

<summary>Does MemoryString connect to Lightroom, Google Photos, or other libraries?</summary>

No. Export or download the photos to a folder on your Mac, then drop that folder into MemoryString.

**Import from Photos** reads the Photos library on your Mac. The app does not sign in to a cloud library.

This stays on your Mac. A live Google Photos or Lightroom connection would send your pictures through someone else's account. That does not fit a private, local app.

[MCP](../mcp.md) can automate the steps on your Mac. It is not a connection to those libraries.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is it free? Do I need an account?</summary>

Yes, it is free. No account. No subscription.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do my photos get uploaded?</summary>

No. Files you add from a folder stay where they are. The show remembers their location.

Photos you import from the Photos app are copied onto your Mac, so the show can still open them. Nothing is sent to a server.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Which Mac do I need?</summary>

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Apple Silicon or Intel.

There is no Windows app and no iPhone app.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What if I can't use the App Store?</summary>

Get it on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/memorystring/id6806579257?mt=12). That is also where you can rate it.

If the Store is not an option, the Mac installer is [here](https://github.com/zackypick/memorystring-downloads/releases/download/v1.0.2/MemoryString-1.0.2.dmg).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why doesn't it reopen my last show?</summary>

On purpose. If a show crashes the app, reopening it would crash again. Use **File → Open** for a saved show.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Where did my exported movie go? It did not start playing.</summary>

MemoryString shows the file in Finder. It does not open a video player.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I remove "Created with MemoryString" at the end?</summary>

The MemoryString signature stays at the end of the movie you share. The app is free, and that signature is how someone watching it hears about MemoryString and can download it too.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is this a photo editor?</summary>

No. It makes the movie. Looks change the feel of the show. They are not a catalog where you retouch each photo.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What happens to photos that are not in the movie?</summary>

They can sit in **Outtakes**. Nothing is deleted. Drag a photo back when you want it in the show.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I use my own music?</summary>

Yes. Add your own audio files, or pick tracks from the built-in royalty-free library. Only add music you have the rights to use.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I automate it?</summary>

Yes. The [MCP server](../mcp.md) runs on your Mac and can drive the app. Use it for scripts and assistants. It does not connect cloud photo libraries.

</details>
