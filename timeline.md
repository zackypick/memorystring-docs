# Timeline

Under the preview: scrub, reorder, trim, and line up slides with music.

![Timeline header: clip name, caption field, Generate, zoom](../.gitbook/assets/timeline-chrome.png)

## Layout

Top to bottom:

1. **Time ruler** — click or drag to seek; hover for a ghost preview
2. **Photo lane** — slides, intro, and group cells
3. **Music lane** — soundtrack clips

Chrome above the lanes shows the selected clip **name + duration** and a zoom control. **1.0×** means the whole project fits the window. Pinch, scroll-wheel zoom, **⌥⌘+** / **⌥⌘-**, or a middle-mouse drag to pan.

Long projects scroll instead of squeezing cells unreadably. During playback the strip follows the playhead.

## Reorder and trim

Drag clips on the photo lane (or from the Library into a gap). Music clips reorder on the music lane. Hold a drag near the left or right edge to auto-scroll.

Select a clip, then drag **edge grips**. Videos have a **2 second** minimum. Right-click a video or music clip:

- **Set Start Here** / **Set End Here**
- **Reset Length**

The Inspector clip footer offers the same playhead trims. **⌘1** / **⌘2** are Set Start / Set End; **⌘D** opens **Set Duration…**.

## Motion → Timeline (Studio)

Inspector → **Motion** → **Timeline**:

- **Sort by Date Taken** — Oldest First / Newest First / Import Order
- **Shuffle Slides**
- **Shuffle Transitions**
- **Reset Slide Durations**

## Groups on the strip

When a multi-photo window is on, follower slides collapse onto a **lead** cell (for example “stack of 4”, “carousel of 5”, “ribbon of N”, “pair of 2”). See [Multi-photo groups](motion/groups.md).
